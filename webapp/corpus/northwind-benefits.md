# Northwind — Employee Benefits Summary

## Time Off

Full-time employees accrue paid time off at 1.5 days per month, or 18 days per year, with a maximum
carryover of 10 unused days into the next calendar year. Sick leave is separate and accrues at one
day per month with no cap. New parents receive 12 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which may be
taken any time within the first year after birth or adoption. Northwind observes eleven paid
holidays per year and closes the office for the last week of December, which does not count against
PTO.

## Health and Retirement

Northwind covers 90% of the medical premium for employees and 70% for dependents. Dental and vision
are fully covered for employees at no cost. The company matches 401(k) contributions dollar for
dollar up to 5% of salary, and matched funds vest immediately with no waiting period. Open
enrollment runs the first two weeks of November, and changes take effect on January 1.

## Learning and Equipment

Every employee has an annual learning budget of 2,000 dollars for courses, books, and conferences,
which does not roll over. Home-office equipment is reimbursed up to 1,500 dollars in the first year
and 500 dollars per year thereafter. Conference travel requires manager approval and is booked
through the company travel portal. Certifications directly relevant to an employee's role are
reimbursed in full and are not counted against the learning budget.

## Remote Work

Northwind is remote-first. Employees may work from anywhere within the countries where the company
has a legal entity: the United States, Canada, and Ireland. Working from another country for more
than 30 days per year requires HR approval for tax reasons. The company sponsors two in-person team
gatherings per year, and travel to those is fully covered.

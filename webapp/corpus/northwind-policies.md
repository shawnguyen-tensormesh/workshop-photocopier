# Northwind Supply Co. — Customer Policies Handbook

## Returns and Refunds

Northwind accepts returns on most items within 30 days of delivery. To be eligible for a full
refund, an item must be unused, in its original packaging, and accompanied by the order number.
Opened items may still be returned within the 30-day window, but a restocking fee of 15% applies
to any item whose factory seal has been broken, except for defective goods. Defective items are
never subject to a restocking fee and are eligible for a full refund or a free replacement at the
customer's choice. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days
after the returned item is received and inspected at our Reno warehouse.

Certain categories are final sale and cannot be returned: perishable goods, custom-cut cabling,
downloadable software licenses, and any item marked "clearance" on the product page. Gift-card
purchases are non-refundable but never expire. If a customer received the wrong item, Northwind
covers all return shipping and expedites the correct item at no charge.

## Shipping and Delivery

Standard ground shipping is free on orders over 75 dollars and takes 3 to 6 business days within
the continental United States. Orders under 75 dollars incur a flat 6.95 shipping charge. Two-day
air is available for 18.50 and next-day air for 34.00; both must be placed before 1 PM Pacific to
ship the same day. Northwind ships to Alaska, Hawaii, and Puerto Rico by air only, and surcharges
apply. We do not currently ship internationally except to Canada, where duties are the customer's
responsibility.

Every shipment includes a tracking number emailed at dispatch. If a package is marked delivered
but not received, customers should file a claim within 14 days so Northwind can open an
investigation with the carrier. Lost-in-transit packages are replaced at no cost once the carrier
confirms the loss.

## Warranty

All Northwind-branded hardware carries a limited 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects,
starting on the delivery date. The warranty covers parts and labor but excludes damage from
misuse, liquid exposure, unauthorized repair, or normal cosmetic wear. Third-party products sold
by Northwind carry only the manufacturer's own warranty. To make a warranty claim, contact support
with the order number and a description of the fault; approved claims are repaired or replaced
within 10 business days of the unit arriving at our service center.

## Support Hours and Contact

The Northwind support desk is open Monday through Friday, 6 AM to 6 PM Pacific, and Saturday from
8 AM to 2 PM Pacific. The desk is closed on Sundays and on federal holidays. Priority support is
available around the clock for customers on the Business Plus plan, with a guaranteed one-hour
response for outage-severity tickets. The general support line is 1-800-555-0142 and the email is
help@northwind.example.

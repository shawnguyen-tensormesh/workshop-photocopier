Honest regime (live, Llama-3.1-8B, measured 2026-07-25): two real wins. (1) Reuse — plain vLLM recomputes the full ~16k-token context for every query (~1s to first token); Tensormesh reuses it from its 170 GB shared tier (~0.2s) — a ~5× first-token win, measured paced (one query at a time). (2) Sharing & persistence — a fresh or restarted engine is warm on arrival, which a single engine's local memory can't do. The throughput multipliers (2–4×, gpt-oss-120b) are on the slides.
